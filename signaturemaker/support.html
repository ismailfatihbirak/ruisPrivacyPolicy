Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a signature?

Open My Signatures and tap New signature. You can draw it with your finger, scan a signature you signed on paper with the camera, or import a photo of it.

How do I sign a document?

Tap Scan to scan a paper document with the camera, or open Documents and tap Add document to import a PDF or photos. Tap Add signature, place it on the page, then tap Done and choose Create signed copy. Your original file is never changed.

How do I share or save a signed document?

Open the signed copy and tap Share or Save to Files. Exporting signed documents requires Premium.

What does Premium include, and how do I cancel?

Premium unlocks sharing and saving signed documents. It is an auto-renewing weekly or yearly subscription purchased through Apple. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple Account settings: open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

I changed my device. How do I restore Premium?

Open Settings in the App and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are my documents and signatures stored?

Only on your device. No account is needed and your documents and signatures are not uploaded to our servers. See the Privacy Policy.

Is a signature made with the App legally binding?

The App creates an image of your handwritten signature and places it on a document. Whether a signed document is accepted depends on the laws and requirements that apply to you and to the recipient; the App does not provide certified or qualified electronic signatures.
