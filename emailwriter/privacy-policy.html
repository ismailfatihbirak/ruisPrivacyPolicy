Privacy Policy

AI Email Writer · Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Email Writer ("Email Writer", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App. If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • Text is sent for AI processing only when you ask. The text you submit (for example notes, pasted emails, or an email thread) is sent to our AI writing service to generate a result only when you tap an AI action. Nothing is sent in the background.
  • Your emails and results are stored on your device. An account is optional and is only used to sync your data across your devices. The App works without an account.
  • Connecting Gmail or Outlook is optional. The App never sees your mailbox password and never sends an email unless you approve it.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not use your text for advertising, and the App shows no third-party advertising and does not track you.
  • We use Firebase for anonymous analytics and crash reporting, and Adapty with Apple for subscriptions.

2. Text You Write and AI Processing

AI Email Writer writes emails from your notes, writes replies to emails you paste or open from a connected mailbox, checks grammar, and refines text (rewrite, shorten, expand, or follow your own instruction), using your tone, length, and language preferences.

  • What is sent: when you tap an AI action, the App sends the text needed for that action to the App's AI writing service: your notes, the pasted email, the email thread when you use AI Reply, the text to correct or refine, and your instruction and preferences. If you switch on your writing profile, the profile details you entered (such as name, role, company, writing context, and sign-off) are included so the result sounds like you. If you leave the writing profile off, these details are not sent.
  • When: only when you tap an AI action. The App does not send your text while you type and does not scan your mailbox in the background.
  • How it is used: the text is used to generate the result you asked for and to operate and protect the service. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.
  • AI provider: the AI writing service is provided by a third party that processes your text on our behalf. It processes your text only to generate the result and send it back to the App.
  • Results: generated emails are saved in My Emails on your device. They are only synced if you sign in to an account (see Section 3).

3. Account and Sync

You can use the App without an account. If you choose to sign in, you can use Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email link. An account is used to sync My Emails and your writing profile across your devices.

  • Account information: your sign-in identifier and the email address and name provided by Apple, Google, or you (Apple may give you the option to hide your email address).
  • Synced data: My Emails and your writing profile are stored on our servers while you are signed in, so that they are available on your other devices.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings › Account › Delete account in the App. This deletes your account and the data synced to it. Copies stored on your devices remain until you delete them or delete the App.

4. Connected Mailboxes (Gmail and Outlook)

You can optionally connect a Gmail or Outlook mailbox. Connecting is done through the provider's own sign-in page (OAuth). The App never sees or stores your mailbox password.

  • What the App does with your mailbox: read messages and threads you open in the App, send emails that you review and approve, save drafts, and move mail to Trash when you ask. The App never sends, saves, or deletes anything on its own.
  • Tokens: access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • AI processing: email content is sent to the AI writing service only when you tap AI Reply or AI Write (see Section 2).
  • Disconnect: you can disconnect at any time in Settings › Connected mailboxes. Disconnecting stops the App's access; nothing is deleted from your mailbox at the provider. You can also revoke access in your Google Account or Microsoft account security settings.

Google API Services User Data Policy. AI Email Writer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Gmail data only to provide and improve the user-facing email features you request in the App (reading messages and threads, drafting replies, sending emails you approve, saving drafts, and moving mail to Trash). We do not sell Gmail data, do not use it for advertising, do not allow humans to read it unless you give consent for specific messages, it is required for security or to comply with law, or the data is aggregated and anonymized for internal operations, and we do not use it to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models. Gmail content is shared with our AI writing service only to generate the result you requested.

Microsoft. For Outlook, the App accesses your mailbox through Microsoft Graph with the permissions you grant on Microsoft's consent screen. We use that data only to provide the features described above, and we do not sell it or use it for advertising. Microsoft handles your account under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

5. Voice Dictation

If you use voice dictation, the App uses Apple's Speech framework to turn your speech into text. Where your device supports it, recognition runs on the device. Otherwise Apple may process the audio under its own terms. The App does not store your audio. iOS asks for microphone and speech recognition permission the first time you use dictation, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings. The resulting text is handled like any other text you write.

6. AI Keyboard (Optional)

The App includes an optional keyboard extension. It asks for Full Access only so that it can reach the AI writing service.

  • Text is sent only when you tap an AI action in the keyboard (Rewrite, Shorten, or Tone), and only the text for that action is sent.
  • Nothing is sent while you type normally, and the keyboard does not log your keystrokes.
  • The keyboard does not work in secure text fields such as passwords; iOS does not allow custom keyboards there.
  • You can turn Full Access off or remove the keyboard at any time in iOS Settings; the rest of the App keeps working.

7. Information Collected Automatically

To understand how the App is used and to fix problems, limited technical information is collected through our service providers. It does not include the content of your emails, notes, or results.

  • Product interaction: anonymous events such as app launches, onboarding progress, screens viewed, features used (for example generating, replying, or checking grammar), and paywall views.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, error information, performance data, app version, iOS version, and device model.
  • Identifiers: app instance identifiers created by Firebase and Adapty. The App does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).
  • Approximate location: a coarse location (such as country or region) may be derived from your IP address by our service providers. The App does not request access to your precise location.
  • Purchase information: subscription status, product identifiers, and transaction status needed to provide Pro features and restore purchases.

8. Subscriptions and Payments

Pro features are offered as auto-renewable subscriptions (yearly, which may include a free trial if you are eligible, and weekly) purchased through Apple. Payments are processed by Apple; we never receive your payment card details. Adapty helps us verify subscription status, show the subscription screen, and restore purchases. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account settings.

9. Third-Party Services

  • AI writing provider: generates emails, replies, and corrections from the text you submit. It processes the text under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Gmail (Google) and Outlook (Microsoft): only if you connect a mailbox. They handle your mailbox under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in: only if you create an account.
  • Firebase (Google): Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, used for usage analytics and crash reporting. See the Firebase privacy information.
  • Adapty: subscription management, paywall display, and purchase validation. See the Adapty Privacy Policy.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, subscriptions, refunds, speech recognition, and system privacy controls. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

10. How We Use Information

  • Provide the App's features, including AI writing, syncing, mailbox actions you request, Pro features, and restoring purchases.
  • Understand which features are used and improve the App.
  • Detect, investigate, and fix crashes, abuse, and technical issues.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Comply with legal obligations and App Store requirements.

11. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use your text for advertising. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above so they can provide their services to us or to you, and with authorities if required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

12. Tracking

The App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, does not use your data for targeted advertising, and does not show ads.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

My Emails and your writing profile remain on your device until you delete them in the App or delete the App. If you use an account, synced copies remain until you delete them or delete your account (Settings › Account › Delete account). Text sent for AI processing is retained by the AI provider only as described in its terms. Analytics, diagnostic, and subscription data are kept by our service providers according to their retention settings and policies. To make a data request, contact us at birak200@gmail.com.

14. Your Choices and Rights

  • You can use the App without an account, and you can delete your account in the App at any time.
  • You can disconnect a mailbox in Settings › Connected mailboxes and revoke access with Google or Microsoft.
  • You can revoke microphone and speech recognition permission, or the keyboard's Full Access, at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete emails and results in the App, or delete the App to remove its local data.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. Contact us at birak200@gmail.com to make a request.

15. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including storing mailbox tokens in the iOS Keychain and encrypting data in transit. Your emails and results also rely on the security of your device, so we recommend using a device passcode. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

16. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address it.

17. International Processing

Our service providers, including the AI provider, may process information in countries other than the one where you live, where data protection laws may differ.
