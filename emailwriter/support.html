Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write an email?

Type or dictate your notes, choose your tone, length and language, and tap the AI action to generate the email. You can then refine it: rewrite, shorten, expand, or give your own instruction. Results are saved in My Emails.

How do I reply to an email?

Paste the email you received, or open it from a connected mailbox, and use AI Reply. The App uses the email, or the whole thread, to draft a reply. Nothing is sent until you review it and approve it.

Does the App send emails by itself?

No. The App only sends an email from your mailbox after you approve it. AI output can be inaccurate, so please review every email before sending.

How do I connect or disconnect Gmail or Outlook?

Connect a mailbox from the App and sign in on the provider's own page; the App never sees your password. To disconnect, open Settings › Connected mailboxes. Disconnecting does not delete anything from your mailbox. You can also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

What data is sent to the AI?

Only the text for the action you tap (for example your notes, the pasted email or thread, or text to correct), plus your writing profile details if you switched the profile on. Nothing is sent while you type, and the text is not used for advertising or sold. See the Privacy Policy.

Do I need an account?

No. The App works without one, and your emails are stored on your device. Signing in with Apple, Google or an email link syncs My Emails and your writing profile across your devices.

How do I delete my account and data?

Open Settings › Account › Delete account. This removes your account and the data synced to it. Emails stored only on your device are removed when you delete them or delete the App.

How do I use voice dictation?

Tap the microphone and allow microphone and speech recognition access when iOS asks. Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition, on the device when supported, and the App does not store your audio. If you turned permission off, re-enable it in iOS Settings.

How do I set up the AI keyboard?

Open iOS Settings › General › Keyboard › Keyboards › Add New Keyboard, choose the AI Email Writer keyboard, then tap it and turn on Allow Full Access. Full Access is needed only to reach the writing service. Text is sent only when you tap an AI action (Rewrite, Shorten or Tone), and the keyboard does not log your keystrokes.

What does Pro include, and how do I cancel?

Pro unlocks the App's premium features. It is an auto-renewing yearly (with a free trial where you are eligible) or weekly subscription purchased through Apple. You can manage or cancel it at any time in your Apple Account settings: open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

I changed my device. How do I restore Pro?

Open Settings in the App and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
